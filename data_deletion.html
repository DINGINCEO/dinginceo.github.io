Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety policies and global privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA), Lumi Adventure Magic Casual provides a straightforward way for users to request the deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

What data will be deleted?

Upon a successful deletion request, we will permanently remove the following information from our servers:

  • Your User Account and Authentication credentials.
  • Linked Email Address and Username.
  • All Game Progression (Coins, Levels, Unlocked Characters).
  • Your Locked Location Data (Region, Country, City).
  • Device identifiers linked to your specific account.

How to request deletion:

To request data deletion, please send an email from the address associated with your game account to our support team:

dinginsceo@gmail.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request - Lumi Adventure
Requirement: Please include your Game Username or User ID (UID) if possible to speed up the process.

Processing Time

Once we receive your request, our team will verify the ownership of the account. Data deletion is usually processed within 7 business days. Once deleted, this action is irreversible and your progress cannot be recovered.
