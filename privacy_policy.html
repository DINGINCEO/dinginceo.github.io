Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 11 February 2025

Welcome to the magic world of Lumi Adventure Magic Casual. This application is operated by DINGIN CEO ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is of the utmost importance to us, and we are dedicated to protecting it with the highest standards of integrity.

This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you interact with our mobile game and describes your privacy rights, including how you can request data deletion.

1. Information We Process

We collect information to provide a better gaming experience, enable competitive leaderboards, and ensure game stability.

A. Personal and Account Data

  • Username: Chosen by you for identification in the game and leaderboards.
  • Email Address: Used for authentication through Google Play Games and technical support.
  • User ID (UID): A unique identifier generated by Firebase to link your progress to your account.

B. One-Time Accurate Location Data

To provide a professional and regionalized leaderboard experience (City, Country, and Global rankings), the app may request access to your device's GPS location. This data is handled with strict privacy protocols:

  • Collection Timing: Location is requested only once during the server selection or registration process. We do not track your location continuously or during regular gameplay.
  • Purpose: To assign you to the correct regional leaderboard (e.g., your specific City and Country) and display your national flag accurately.
  • Data Locking: Once your location is accurately detected and locked in our database, it cannot be changed. This prevents "server hopping" and ensures competitive integrity across all regions.

C. Game Progression and Performance

  • Mana (Coins) balance, High Scores, and Player Rank.
  • Character collection status and level-based unlocks.
  • Total online time used for ranking eligibility and "Gelar" (Title) progression.
  • Season reset data (last reset season ID).

D. Device and Technical Information

  • Device Model, Manufacturer, and Android Version.
  • Unique Device Identifier (Android ID) used strictly for fraud prevention and duplicate account detection.

2. Third-Party Integration

We partner with industry-leading service providers to support our game's functionality.

  • Google AdMob: For delivering relevant advertisements. Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Firestore & Analytics): For secured cloud storage, progression synchronization, and technical usage analysis. Data Practices
  • Google Play Games Services: For secure authentication and global cloud saves.

3. Data Retention and Season Resets

Game rankings (Coins) are reset every 6 months (on July 2nd and December 2nd) to keep the competition fresh. While ranking data is reset, your account identification and locked regional data are retained until you request deletion.

4. Data Deletion Rights

In compliance with Google Play's Data Safety policies, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data. You can initiate this process by visiting our Data Deletion Request Page or by emailing us directly.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our game is designed to be safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate legal safeguards and parental consent where required.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Significant changes will be noted in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
