Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 27 Mei 2026

Welcome to the magic world of Lumi Adventure Magic Casual. This application is operated by DINGIN CEO ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is of the utmost importance to us, and we are dedicated to protecting it with the highest standards of integrity.

This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you interact with our mobile game and describes your privacy rights.

1. Information We Process

We collect minimal data to ensure a smooth gaming experience, synchronize your progress across devices, and maintain game stability.

A. Local & Cloud Game Data

We use local storage (SharedPreferences) and Firebase Firestore to manage your progression. This includes:

  • Mana (Coins) balance and achievements.
  • Character collection status (Ninja, Royal, King, and Standard Lumi).
  • In-game power-up inventory.
  • World exploration progress and level-based unlocks.
  • High scores and Player Rank.

B. Device Information

To provide technical support and prevent fraud, we may process:

  • Device Model and Android Version.
  • Unique Device Identifier (Android ID).
  • Firebase User ID (if signed in via Play Games).

2. Third-Party Integration

To support our game and analyze performance, we partner with industry-leading service providers.

  • Google AdMob: For delivering advertisements. They may use device identifiers to provide relevant ads and log ad-related events (impressions, clicks) to help us optimize the experience.
    Review Google's Privacy Standards
  • Firebase (Analytics & Firestore): We use Firebase to analyze gameplay patterns, store game progress for synchronization, and log technical ad events. This data helps us improve game stability and balance.
    Review Firebase Data Practices
  • Google Play Games Services: Used for cloud saves and authentication.
    Review Google's Privacy Policy

3. Global Compliance (GDPR)

For players in the European Economic Area (EEA), we strictly adhere to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data.

You can clear your data by uninstalling the app or contacting us to request the deletion of your cloud-synced progress linked to your account.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Lumi Adventure Magic Casual is a safe environment for all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate safeguards.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security protocols to safeguard your data. While no digital system is 100% secure, we strive to protect your progress and information from unauthorized access.

6. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy. Any revisions will be signified by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
